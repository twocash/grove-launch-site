## Site Structure

### Page 1: Home / Thesis — "The Architecture Is the Policy"
- G7 divergence argument (6 of 7 building sovereign, US consolidating)
- Centralized architectures are bad CS (latency, cost, SPOF, no auditability)
- Vendor dependency (deprecation cycles, TOS changes, telemetry exploitation)
- Physics don't cooperate (Stargate Abilene failure, grid politics)
- Architecture constrains what policy can achieve (telemetry as competitive intelligence)
- 150-node distributed vs centralized visualization (interactive, from current preview hub)
- Epistemic capture argument ("When you become the knowledge surface")

### Page 2: The Λ Landscape — "Measuring What Nobody Else Measures"
- Live Lambda standings (updated with Gemma 4 when available)
- Methodology summary (power law, geometric mean β, V multiplier)
- Conflict of interest disclosure (Grove scores own pattern lowest)
- Dependency Profile vs Sovereignty Profile framing
- Links to full methodology PDF

### Page 3: The Autonomaton Pattern — "Open Standard 001"
- Standards publication page (IETF/W3C aesthetic, not product launch)
- Five-stage pipeline overview
- Zone model (Green/Yellow/Red)
- Ratchet mechanism
- CC BY 4.0 license
- Full spec: designed HTML version + PDF download
- Call for reviewers

### Page 4: The Foundation — "What We Are"
- Standards body modeled after the Linux Foundation
- Mission: open, inspectable, capture-resistant architecture for cognitive sovereignty
- Three-act roadmap sketch (Autonomaton → Trellis → Knowledge Commons)
- Team / advisory board (when formalized)
