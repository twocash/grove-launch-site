The vector — read every card through it

In the SaaS era, the contractual bucket “Usage Data” meant benign operational exhaust — uptime, latency, crash logs. In the agentic era the same category, often the same words, now carries the harness's high-value telemetry: accept/reject reward labels, edit-distance, embeddings and vectors of the codebase, metadata, outcome verification. Each card documents (1) the ownership / retention / definition clause and (2) what non-SaaS-era artifacts now populate that bucket. Fact is separated from interpretation; no scope or legal conclusion is asserted.
