## Keystone claim for this sprint

> *The operator is a Keg at the substrate layer.*

GRV-004 as currently published treats lineage as artifact-to-artifact predecessor attribution. The Jim Calhoun Keg surfaces a structural extension: an operator can declare their own substrate — the humans, the artifacts, the moments, the ground — as a Keg in their Autonomaton's cellar. The declaration is first-person, memoir-register, Workshop-adjacent where provisional. Scope is deliberately limited: this is *one operator's substrate declaration*, not a template, not a requirement, not a prescription for how every grove gets composed.

The structural move is that the operator who ships a conforming Autonomaton is themselves substrate for the Mesh. Other operators MAY declare themselves via a memoir-register Keg. Most will not. The canon holds either way.

This Keg is the first instance on record.
