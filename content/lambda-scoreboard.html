What this measures

The question nobody else asks

$650 billion is being deployed into AI infrastructure. Analyst frameworks — Gartner, Forrester, IDC — measure how much money flows in. None of them measure a structural question: does this architecture propagate on its own merits, or does it require continuous capital subsidy and vendor distribution power to sustain adoption?

The Λ framework isolates that structural question. It draws on technology diffusion theory — Bass curves, Granovetter cascades, Schelling tipping points — to model whether a deployment pattern has reached the threshold where adoption becomes self-reinforcing. Technology adoption is not linear. It follows threshold dynamics where small changes in friction produce outsized changes in adoption. A pattern scoring 0.03 isn’t 30× worse than one scoring 1.0 — it exists in a fundamentally different regime. The tiers are not rankings. They are phase states.

The methodology
Λ = (S × R × V) / (1 + (β · Fc)²)
S
Spreadability
How freely replicated. Open licensing scores high.
R
Standardized Rails
Infrastructure fit. Well-paved integration paths.
V
Validation
Discounts theory. Pre-deployment: 0.2. Validated: 1.0.
Fc
Cognitive Friction
Adoption resistance. Squared in the denominator.
β
Exogenous Incentive
External forcing. Geometric mean of financial, regulatory, ideological.

S, R, and V are linear — improve them 10% and the score moves 10%. But Fc and β are squared in the denominator. Reduce friction by half and adoption doesn’t double. It quadruples. The geometry of the fight matters more than the size of the sword.

The structural insight

Dependency compounds. Sovereignty compounds.

Dependency Profile
High integration ease. Low spreadability. Easy to adopt, hard to leave. Each quarter of adoption accumulates switching costs — proprietary weights, vendor-controlled deprecation, captured telemetry.
OpenAI · Google Gemini · Microsoft Copilot · Anthropic Claude
Every quarter on a centralized API is another quarter of vendor leverage.
Sovereignty Profile
High spreadability. Higher adoption friction. Harder to adopt, easier to leave. Each quarter of adoption builds structural independence — open weights, portable architecture, sovereign telemetry.
Mistral / DeepSeek · Autonomaton
Every quarter on an open architecture is another quarter of independence.

Which profile wins depends on one question: does the financial and regulatory environment over the next 3–4 years favor continued centralization, or does it reward architectural independence? Six of seven G7 nations have made their bet.
