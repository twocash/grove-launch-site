## Nuances to be aware of

- **UK is hybrid:** Sovereign AI Unit + distributed systems funding, but ALSO Stargate UK/OpenAI deal and MoJ becoming OpenAI's first UK gov customer. Direction of travel is toward sovereignty but they're hedging.
- **Japan is dual-track:** Sovereign domestic models AND SoftBank's massive OpenAI/Stargate investment. Policy direction is unambiguous toward domestic capability.
- **Canada's tension:** Flagship $240M data center operated by CoreWeave (US company). "Asserts sovereignty through physical presence while conceding legal and operational control."
- **France is the clearest case:** Open-weight, sovereign infrastructure, explicit rejection of US vendor dependency. Mistral as national champion with Apache 2.0 licensing.
- **US "deregulation" framing is misleading:** Commerce Dept took 9.9% equity stake in Intel. $10B+ in equity positions across semiconductors. This is industrial policy disguised as deregulation.
