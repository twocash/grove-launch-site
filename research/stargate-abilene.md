# Stargate Abilene — Hyperscale Physics Failure
## Research brief for Beat 1, Section 3
**Compiled:** April 7, 2026

---

## Timeline
- **Jan 21, 2025:** Trump announces Stargate at White House. $500B, 20 data centers, 10 GW.
- **Sep 2025:** Two buildings operational at Abilene (~200 MW). Six more under construction.
- **Nov 2025:** Crusoe tops out final building. 1.2 GW campus target mid-2026.
- **Early 2026:** Winter weather disrupts liquid cooling, multiple buildings offline for days.
- **Mar 4, 2026:** Crusoe SVP tells Abilene city officials campus was built around under-utilized Texas grid capacity.
- **Mar 6, 2026:** Bloomberg reports 600 MW expansion cancelled. Financing collapsed.
- **Mar 9, 2026:** Oracle stock drops. Azure begins auto-upgrading GPT-4o to GPT-5.1.

## Key Facts
- Abilene campus: ~$15B, most expensive data center ever financed
- Operator: Crusoe Energy Systems (pivoted from flare gas Bitcoin mining)
- Crusoe CEO Chase Lochmiller: publicly acknowledged "learn a lot on the job"
- Oracle debt: $100B+ for Stargate commitments, free cash flow negative
- SoftBank: seeking $40B in loans for its share
- Oracle pursued $38B debt package (Oct 2025) — largest in company history
- 14 months post-announcement: 200 MW operational out of 10 GW promised (2%)
- GPU power density: 120-140 kW per rack, individual GPUs 700-1,200 watts
- 10 GW = enough to power ~7.5 million homes

## Political / Grid Constraints
- Michigan: Oracle must pay $500M grid upgrades, curtailed first in shortages, take-or-pay clause
- Texas SB6: Data centers can be cut off in grid emergencies
- Texas grid demand forecast to double in six years
- 2021 Winter Storm Uri killed 246 people, left millions without power

## The Nvidia Angle
- Nvidia paid $150M deposit to Crusoe to block AMD chips from Abilene
- Nvidia brokering Meta as replacement tenant for abandoned expansion capacity
- Chip manufacturers now actively participating in data center real estate decisions

## Strategic Vulnerability (for "no single address" argument)
- 1.2 GW = more electricity than a mid-sized city
- Single facility houses inference capacity for thousands of downstream applications
- No redundancy, no fallback at that concentration level
- Distributed architectures: no single facility, no single grid connection, no single operator
